Freelancer.com

Global marketplace

Across six global product teams, I worked on the connective experiences that determine whether a marketplace can grow: trusted entry, relevant discovery, and a hiring flow that gives both clients and freelancers confidence to proceed.

Scope

Onboarding and KYC · Marketplace discovery · Unified search · Freelancer profiles · Hiring funnels · User research

Role

Senior Product Designer

Teams

Six global product teams

The challenge

Growth depended on confidence at every step.

For new users, account verification could feel like a barrier before they experienced the marketplace's value. For clients ready to hire, search exposed a large inventory of talent but did not always make relevance, quality, or next actions easy to judge.

We needed to improve the complete path: help people join with confidence, surface the right people and work, and make the decision to hire feel clear enough to act on.

Onboarding and trust

Growth foundation

Verification became easier to understand and finish.

I redesigned onboarding and KYC around a clearer sequence of decisions, requirements, and progress. The experience made the purpose of verification more visible while reducing uncertainty about what users needed to provide next.

The resulting flow increased verified accounts by 35% within three months, giving more people a trusted starting point in the marketplace.

One search, many intentions

Marketplace discovery

Search was redesigned around how people decide.

Clients were not simply looking for a keyword match. They were weighing skills, reputation, availability, rate, location, portfolio evidence, and the confidence to start a conversation. I helped shape a unified search model that brought freelancers, jobs, projects, and contests into a clearer, more consistent discovery experience.

The information architecture separated broad exploration from focused filtering, while result cards gave people the evidence needed to compare talent without losing momentum.

Making hiring easier to complete

Core experience

Relevant information became a clearer path to action.

Search and hiring work focused on the moments where clients stalled: interpreting profiles, narrowing choices, and understanding how to start a working relationship. The goal was not to add more information, but to make the right information easier to recognize and act on.

Improving those paths reduced support tickets related to hiring difficulty by 35%. The redesigned hourly funnel also increased revenue by 60% within six months.

Research kept the work grounded

Interviews and usability studies with newly registered users, returning users, and experienced freelancers helped the teams identify where marketplace language, filters, badges, and ranking cues were unclear. Findings became product decisions through collaborative mapping, prototyping, and iterative testing.

Small reductions in friction created measurable marketplace growth.

What I learned

Trust is a product outcome, not just a compliance step.

Onboarding has to explain value as clearly as it asks for effort. Clear requirements and visible progress help people move through verification with greater confidence.

Search is a decision system.

People need more than results. They need understandable signals, useful comparisons, and a clear next step that helps them choose with confidence.

Marketplace growth happens across connected moments.

Improving a single surface is rarely enough. Onboarding, discovery, profiles, and hiring must work together to turn attention into successful matches.
