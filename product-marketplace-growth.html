Challenge

Context

Three connected points in the journey created distinct growth challenges.

01 - Establish trust (Onboarding & KYC)

Freelancers needed to complete profile setup and verification before they could build a credible marketplace presence.

02 - Find a match (Discover and Search)

Employers often knew the outcome they wanted, but not the skills or keywords needed to find the right freelancer.

03 - Start the work (Hiring Experience)

Employers needed to evaluate proposals, understand milestones, and choose a hiring model that fit the project.

Process

Cross-functional Collaboration

Treat the funnels as connected, but investigate and measure each one on its own terms.

I mapped the member journey from account setup through discovery to hiring, partnering with product managers, lead engineers, QA leads, and data scientists across multiple teams. We identified pain points in each funnel and examined where people dropped off, disengaged, struggled to verify their accounts, or had difficulty finding relevant talent.

I was embedded with product teams to design end-to-end experiences across freelancer onboarding, employer discovery, and core hiring.

Current Search Behaviour

Search funnel

Search was split across separate 3 experiences.

  • Employers moved between different pages to find freelancers, jobs, and contests.
  • Inconsistent patterns and ranking behaviour made it harder to compare results and understand which path would help them find the right match.

UX Research

Search Funnel

It takes 6 minutes to complete the search process (finding a freelancer, jobs)

We tested 2 starting points, their task was to find a part-time freelancer for a project such as creating a brochure. The study also explored the desktop and mobile search experiences.

What marketplace behaviour revealed

  • 17% of search calls included project keywords.
  • Among keyword searches, 84% included skills, suggesting many employers were translating their need into platform terminology.
  • Employers could find freelancers but lacked enough relevant information to confidently compare fit.
  • Newly registered and verified freelancers were not consistently visible in results.

Solutions

3 funnel workstreams

Improve each critical transition without treating the marketplace as 1 generic funnel.

Each funnel had a different user need and design response. The 3 solution sections below show how I addressed freelancer trust, employer discovery, and project completion as connected but distinct parts of the marketplace journey.

One marketplace journey, with different needs at each step.

01 - Onboarding and KYC

Establish trust and credibility

Make account setup clear while establishing a credible profile.

Onboarding and KYC

  • I worked on the signup and KYC journey to connect account type, skills, profile details, linked accounts, and identity checks into a more coherent setup path.
  • The design direction clarified individual versus corporate freelancer accounts and placed verification in the context of building a trusted profile, with the goal of strengthening profile quality and trust across the community.

Payment Compliance for Australia, California and EU users

To comply with regional regulations, we extended verification beyond profile completion by making the requirements for sending and receiving payments clear. Depending on the payment provider and applicable policy, employers may need individual or business verification before funding a project, while freelancers may need identity and payout-account checks before withdrawing funds. Business verification may also require entity details or beneficial-owner checks.

Following the onboarding work, verified accounts increased by 35% within 3 months.

This is a team outcome reported alongside the funnel, not an isolated measure of a single screen.

02 - Find a match

Discovery and Search

Unified Search and Creating Discover (Community Pages ).

I explored a more coherent search experience across freelancers, jobs, projects, and contests, while retaining the skills-led path for employers who knew what to search for. The direction paired broader intent with personalized recommendations and filters, so employers could move from an outcome they understood toward relevant talent and work.

Discover (Community Pages)

To make comparison more useful, the experience brought portfolio examples and community work alongside skills, location, rate, and reputation signals. The goal was to improve freelancer discoverability and help employers assess fit without relying on keywords alone.

Related discovery work reported an 11% increase in profile engagement and an 8% increase in portfolio engagement. These are team outcomes across Growth, Core, and Search, not results attributed to the search interface alone.

03 - Start the work

Hiring Experience

Help employers move from comparing proposals to managing work with confidence.

Employers could feel overwhelmed comparing bids and deciding how to structure a project. Incomplete or unpaid milestones could also put project completion at risk. I worked on the hiring path to make project type, budget, and fixed-price versus hourly work clearer during setup, reducing ambiguity about how to begin.

Connect verification to the hiring moment: Before an employer funds a project or milestone, surface any required verification and provide a clear next step. For freelancers, show payment and payout readiness before they initiate a withdrawal. Keep status visible across hiring and payments, explain missing information or review delays, and offer a way to correct or resubmit details. Apply checks only when required by the relevant payments and compliance policies.

The broader journey map also identified needs after the hire: project management, milestone payments, time tracking, and two-way feedback ratings. These are connected opportunities to support project completion and build trust between employers and freelancers; the map describes the direction, not a measured effect for each capability.

For a future iteration, I would track verification completion and time-to-verify alongside funded-project completion, payout success, payment holds, and verification-related support contacts. These are proposed measures, not outcomes from the work described here.

Related hiring work reduced hiring-related support tickets by 35%. The hourly hiring funnel increased revenue by 60% within 6 months.

These are team outcomes reported for the wider hiring workstream.

Learnings

Let employers describe the job before asking for the skill.

Service marketplaces should bridge plain-language needs to freelancer categories and specialties, then let employers refine their search.

Prove relevance in the result.

Skills and reputation are useful signals; portfolios and recent work help employers understand what a freelancer can actually deliver.

Trust is part of the marketplace journey.

Freelancer onboarding, verification, discovery, and hiring influence each other. Measure each intervention clearly, then use the full journey to find where confidence and conversion break down.
